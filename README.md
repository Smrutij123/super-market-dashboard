# super-market-dashboard
The Global Superstore 2016 dataset contains multi-year transactional records spanning global retail operations from January 1, 2012, through December 31, 2015. The dataset captures $12,642,501.91 in overall revenue, representing 25,728 unique customer orders across 147 countries and 7 major international markets.

The workbook (global_superstore_2016.xlsx) consists of three sheets:
1.Orders: Details individual line items per order, including order dates, shipping priority, product attributes (Category, Sub-Category, Name), customer demographics (Segment, City, State, Country, Market), and financial metrics (Sales, Quantity, Discount, Profit, Shipping Cost).

2.Returns: Records returned order IDs along with their respective region.

3.People: Maps assigned regional managers to specific geographical territories.

**Executive  & Key Results**

Performance Breakdown
**1. Performance by Product Category**
-Technology was the primary driver of profitability, generating $4,744,557.32 in sales and $663,778.73 in profit with the highest margin (13.99%).

-Office Supplies yielded high volume (108,244 units sold) with strong margins (13.69%), totaling $518,595.83 in profit.

-Furniture recorded substantial revenue ($4,110,452.07), but suffered from lower overall margins (6.94%), resulting in $285,082.73 in net profit due to high shipping and material costs.

**2. Performance by Market Region**

- Europe led all global markets in profitability with a 13.68% profit margin ($449,551.72 net profit on $3,287,335.85 sales).
- USCA (United States & Canada) followed closely with a 12.87% margin ($304,214.41 profit).
- Asia Pacific (APAC) produced the largest individual sales volume at $4,042,658.58, maintaining a profit margin of 9.97%.
